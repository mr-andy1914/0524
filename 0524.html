What DoN doesn't say enough

So I'll try to say it properly.

पिर्य Doraemon/ Alien,

I'm not very good at saying feelings out loud. You already know that. I'm much better at teasing you, making fun of you, annoying you, and acting like everything is a joke.

But sometimes I stop and think about how weird and funny our story actually is.

Same college. Different sections. Same bus. Different seats. Same colony. And somehow only about two minutes walking distance apart.

Then one evening you got off the bus. I noticed you, went after you, and followed you toward home. You noticed. And instead of walking normally, you walked slower.

I still think that's one of the funniest and cutest things about us. You basically made it easier for me and pretended you didn't. 😂

Then somehow, within the first week, we had our first kiss. A bicycle, a meeting, and suddenly our story had officially started.

And then came Pokhara. We flew there and came back by bus, because apparently we had to experience every possible form of transportation together.

You love flowers. I keep telling you duplicate flowers are better. I know that's not exactly the romantic answer you were hoping for. 😂 But I still owe you real flowers.

You drink coffee. I drink tea. You like vanilla ice cream. You like black outfits on me, and somehow red looks dangerously good on you.

These are tiny things. But I remember them. Maybe I don't say that enough.

The hardest part now is that we went from being two minutes apart to being thousands of kilometres apart. Nepal to Canada is a much longer walk than that old bus stop.

I miss the ordinary moments more than anything. Just being beside you. Going somewhere without needing a plan. Hearing you talk about random things. Annoying you without a phone between us.

I don't always say it properly, but I care about you deeply. I love you. And even after all these years, I still want a future with you.

Our story wasn't perfect or carefully planned. It was a bus ride, a slow walk, a bicycle, a first kiss, a trip to Pokhara, a lot of laughing, a lot of teasing, and now a lot of distance.

But somehow, it's still us.

And if I had to choose all over again...

I'd still follow you home from that bus stop. Every single time.
